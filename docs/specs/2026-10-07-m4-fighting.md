# Milestone 4: fighting on foot

Status: written 07-10-2026 while Mark was away. Every choice below is Claude's default.

## In one paragraph

Pirates now have health and two weapons: a cutlass and a flintlock pistol. Skeletons guard every
treasure island and crew every skeleton ship. They walk up to you and swing their swords. Crews
can board a skeleton ship by its ladders and fight the skeletons on its deck. Fall in a fight and
you wait a few seconds as a ghost, then come back aboard your own ship. Bananas from the barrel on
the lower deck heal you.

## Glossary

| Word | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Health | 100 points. Shown as a bar at the bottom. |
| Cutlass | Melee: left click swings, hitting everything in front of you within reach. |
| Pistol | One shot, then a reload. Hits the first thing along your aim. |
| Skeleton | An enemy on foot. Island skeletons keep to dry land; ship skeletons keep to their deck. |
| Ghost | A fallen player: no moving for 8 s, then back on deck at full health. |
| Boarding | Climbing an enemy ship's ladder. The ship's own frame carries you, as on your own ship. |

## What players do

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Switch weapon | 1 cutlass, 2 pistol |
| Attack | Left click (not while at a station; at a cannon left click still fires the cannon) |
| Eat | E at the banana barrel on the lower deck: +50 health, uses one banana from the ship's stores |
| Board | Swim to the skeleton ship and E at its ladder |

## Rules

- Cutlass: 25 damage, 2.4 m reach, a 70° arc, one swing every 0.6 s.
- Pistol: 40 damage, 60 m range, 2.5 s reload, endless shot.
- Skeletons: 60 health (three cuts or two shots). They walk at 3 m/s and chase anyone within
  30 m of them. A swing does 20 damage within 1.8 m, once every 1.3 s.
- Island camps: three skeletons per treasure island. They wake when a pirate comes within 80 m
  and come back 3 minutes after the last one falls.
- Skeleton ships carry three skeletons on deck, who fight boarders.
- Cannonballs hurt people too: 70 damage within 1.5 m of where they land, 30 within 3.5 m.
- The ship carries 10 bananas (up to 20), restocked with the other supplies.
- Friendly fire is off: you cannot hurt your own crew (crew vs crew comes in milestone 5).

## Also in this milestone

Players can now stand on any ship (ready for crew-vs-crew in milestone 5). Stations, the map
table and the hatch only work on your own ship.
