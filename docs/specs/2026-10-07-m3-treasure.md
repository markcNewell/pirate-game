# Milestone 3: treasure hunting

Status: written 07-10-2026 while Mark was away. Every choice below is Claude's default.

## In one paragraph

The crew takes a voyage at the map table on the quarterdeck and gets a treasure map: an island's
outline with an X. They sail there, go ashore, dig at the X, and carry the chest back to the ship.
They sail to the outpost, Port Merrow, and sell it to the merchant for gold. Cannonballs and planks
are now limited ship supplies. They are restocked from supply barrels at the outpost or fished out
of the sea, and a sunk skeleton ship leaves a captain's chest and supply barrels floating behind.

## Glossary

| Word | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Voyage | One treasure hunt: which island, where the X is. One at a time per crew. |
| Map | What the HUD shows on M: the island's outline, its name and the X. |
| Chest | Treasure you carry in both hands. Three kinds: castaway (~100 gold), seafarer (~250), captain's (~600). |
| Supply barrel | Opened where it lies: gives the ship cannonballs and planks. |
| Outpost | Port Merrow, a new island with a pier, a merchant's hut and supply barrels. |
| Purse | The crew's shared gold. Nothing to spend it on yet. |

## What players do

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Take a voyage | E at the map table beside the wheel. |
| Read the map | Hold M. |
| Dig | Stand on an island and hold E for 2 s. Within 3 m of the X, a chest comes up; elsewhere, nothing. |
| Carry a chest | E to pick it up. You walk and swim slower, and can't use stations. E puts it down (on deck it stays aboard). |
| Sell | Carry a chest to the merchant at Port Merrow and press E. |
| Restock | E at a supply barrel (on the outpost's pier, or floating after a fight) while your ship is within 60 m. |

## Rules

- The ship starts with 30 cannonballs and 15 planks (it can hold up to 60 and 30). Each shot uses
  one cannonball and each patch one plank. Skeleton ships have endless shot.
- An outpost barrel refills the ship to full. A floating barrel adds 15 cannonballs and 8 planks.
- Chests on deck ride with the ship. If the ship sinks they float up where they were. Floating
  loot drifts with the waves and disappears after 10 minutes.
- A sunk skeleton ship leaves a captain's chest and two supply barrels.
- Selling a voyage's chest ends the voyage, and the next one can be taken.

## Not in this milestone

Shovels and other tools as items (digging uses bare hands), spending gold, other crews stealing
chests (milestone 5), skeletons guarding treasure (milestone 4).

## Done when

1. A bot takes a voyage, swims to the island, digs up the chest, carries it back aboard and puts
   it on deck.
2. A bot carries a chest from the deck to the merchant and the purse goes up.
3. Firing and patching use up supplies, and a barrel restocks them.
4. A sunk skeleton ship leaves loot.
5. Unit tests cover items, voyages and digging; the smoke test covers the treasure loop.
