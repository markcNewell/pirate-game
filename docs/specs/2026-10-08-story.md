# The story: the Ash Fleet

Status: written 08-10-2026, after Mark asked for "an overarching story line where the Spanish
are trying to raise the dead and creating an army of skeleton people", running behind the
missions and ending in big battles and bosses. Every name, chapter and number below is
Claude's draft; change anything and the game follows. All of it is built (one spec per milestone,
M7-M12), with the simplifications each milestone's spec lists.

## In one paragraph

Thirty years ago a Spanish treasure fleet sank on the Black Reef the same night it pulled a
strange stone out of the sea: the **Heart of Ash**, which burns with green fire and wakes the
dead. One young officer lived: **Alonso de Cuervo**. Now he is the **Raven Admiral**, back in
the Merrow Isles with the King's alchemist, **Doctor Ocaso**, and the Heart. They are digging
up pirate gold to feed its fire, raising dead pirate captains as officers, and building up to
one night: the night they raise the whole drowned fleet and its two thousand dead. The crews
of Port Merrow fight them chapter by chapter, from a few green lights on Gull Isle to a siege
of the port, a fleet battle in a storm, and a giant made of bones rising out of a whirlpool.

## Glossary

| Word | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Chapter | One story mission. A crew plays them in order; each has a few steps. |
| Act | A run of chapters ending in a big battle with a boss. There are five, plus a prologue. |
| Tale | A short side story from one of the Port Merrow characters (2-3 chapters). |
| Board mission | The repeatable missions from the map table (treasure, bounty, cargo). They keep going all through the story. |
| World event | Something on the shared sea any crew can join: a Spanish patrol, a fort, a raid on Merrow. |
| Call to arms | The three biggest battles ask every crew on the server to come and help. |
| Pirate level | Your own level, from XP. It shows who you are; it never makes you stronger. |
| Deed | A long-running goal ("sink 25 Spanish galleons") that earns a title or something to wear. |
| The dead | Skeletons. Pirate dead are the old island skeletons; Spanish dead wear helmets and breastplates. |

## The world and its people

**The Merrow Isles**, 1716. A ring of small islands round Port Merrow, the one free port.
Pirates have buried their gold here for a hundred years, and buried each other too.

### Port Merrow (the good folk)

| Who | Company | What they're like | Their part |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Silas Penny** | the Gilded Hand | Old, greedy, kind underneath. Sat at his stall for 40 years. Was a cabin boy once. | Buys chests. Learns the Heart eats gold and buys up every coin to starve it. |
| **Mother Wren** | the Lantern Order | Hooded, quiet, dry as bones. Her green lantern shows the souls of the dead. | Buys skulls and lays them to rest so they can't be raised. Knows the old stories. |
| **Abel Quill** | the Merrow Traders | Fussy clerk in a white shirt; loves a ledger more than people. Braver than he looks. | Runs cargo. Salt, oil and powder become the port's war supplies. |
| **Jonah Salt** | (castaway trader) | Cheerful, sunburnt, talks to a coconut called Captain Coco. | Takes deliveries on far islands; sees everything from his beach. |
| **Moll Tinder** | (lighthouse keeper) | Sharp-eyed, short-tempered, never sleeps. | First to see the green lights. Her lighthouse matters in the siege. |
| **Big Martha** | (the Drowned Rat tavern) | Runs the tavern, settles fights with a ladle. | Where the crews meet, plan and celebrate. |
| **Isabel Montero** | (Spanish deserter) | Lieutenant of marines. Proud, honest, angry. | Deserts when she sees what Cuervo is doing. Becomes the crews' best ally. |

### The Spanish (the bad folk)

| Who | What they're like | Their part |
|---|---|---|
| **Alonso de Cuervo, the Raven Admiral** | Fifty, grey, black raven cloak. The only survivor of the drowned fleet. Wants his fleet back and his name restored. | The main enemy. Flagship: the *Cuervo Negro*. Boss of Act IV; rises again in Act V. |
| **Doctor Tomás Ocaso** | The King's alchemist. Thin, spectacles of green glass, a coat full of bottles. Polite. Mad. | Works the Heart. Secretly serves the Heart, not Spain. Boss of Act V. |
| **Sargento Calavera** | A raised Spanish sergeant, big, with a tower shield and a halberd. | Runs the dig camps. Boss of Act I. |
| **El Polvorín** ("the Powder Keg") | A barrel-chested raised gunner who throws lit bombs. | Holds the fort. Boss of Act II. |
| **La Viuda** ("the Widow") | Capitana Elena Robles, raised sharpshooter in a black veil, with a very long musket. | Picks off defenders in the siege. Boss of Act III. |
| **The Pressed Dead** | Famous pirate captains dug up and raised as Spanish officers. | The bounty captains. Black-Eyed Bess and Captain Hollowbell have story parts. |

### How the magic works (kept simple)

- The **Heart of Ash** is a stone heart the size of a barrel, burning with green fire.
- Its fire wakes the dead near it, and the dead obey whoever holds it.
- **Gold feeds it.** The more gold it burns, the further its fire reaches. That is why the Spanish
  dig up pirate hoards, and why every chest sold to Silas is a chest the Heart doesn't eat.
- **A captain's skull makes a captain.** Common bones make common skeletons; the skull of a
  great captain makes an officer. That is why Wren buys skulls and lays them to rest.
- **Salt and seawater put the green fire out.** That is why Quill's cargo becomes war supplies,
  and why buckets of seawater matter.
- The Heart came out of **the Maw**, a whirlpool at the heart of the Black Reef. The only way to
  end it is to give it back.

## The story, act by act

Every chapter below has: the story, what you do (in game terms), and anything new it brings.
Chapters are given by a character at Port Merrow (talk to them) or appear on the mission board's
new "Story" card. A failed chapter goes back to its last finished step.

### Prologue: Green Fire on Gull Isle

*No level needed. Teaches the story's tools with things the game already has.*

**P1. Strange Lights.** Moll has seen green lights on Gull Isle three nights running and nobody
believes her. Sail over (at night if you like). On the beach: fresh-dug graves, a dead Spanish
sailor with a journal, and a green fire burning on a grave. Douse it with a bucket of seawater;
the dead climb out as it hisses. Fight them off and bring the journal to Moll.
*You:* sail, find the journal, douse 1 fire, fight 3 skeletons, talk to Moll.
*New:* talking to characters, journal pages, green fires you douse with the bucket.

**P2. Black-Eyed Bess.** Mother Wren reads the journal by her lantern: "The Doctor says the fire
needs gold, and a captain's skull to make a captain. Tonight, the grave of Black-Eyed Bess."
Bess is already up and walking on Parrot Rock, with the Spanish dead around her. Beat her and
bring her skull to Wren before the Spanish carry it off.
*You:* a bounty with a story (as today's bounties), plus two Spanish skeletons trying to grab
the skull and run for their rowing boat.
*New:* Spanish skeletons (helmet and breastplate); enemies that pick up loot and run.

### Act I: Bones in the Sand

*The first signs. Spanish dig camps, raised pirates, the first raven sails.*

**1. Dig Faster.** Silas: "Somebody is digging up the old hoards, and it isn't my customers."
He sells you a map with three X's on Cutlass Cay. Spanish dead are digging the same spots. Get
there first; if they dig a chest up, they carry it to their boat, so chase them down.
*You:* a three-chest treasure race. *New:* enemy diggers.

**2. Salt the Earth.** Quill has read the journal too, and he has an idea that involves his
favourite thing: inventory. Salt keeps the dead in the ground. Carry three crates of salt to the
old pirate graveyard on Gull Isle and pour one on each open grave, while the dead keep climbing
out of the others.
*You:* cargo to a graveyard, pour (hold E) while defending. *New:* the graveyard, pouring.

**3. Captain Coco's Warning.** Jonah Salt saw a black ship with a gold raven on its sails anchor
off Cutlass Cay, and men rowing powder ashore. Follow his directions ("where the two rocks kiss,
twenty paces toward the sunset") to a Spanish powder store. Light a keg, run, and blow it up.
Inside the wreckage: orders signed *A. de C.*
*You:* a riddle clue, carrying a lit powder keg. *New:* riddle directions, powder kegs that
explode (they hurt you too).

**4. The Raven Sails.** The black ship is back: a Spanish galleon crewed by the dead, raven on
its sails. Sink it, and fish its captain's chest out of the wreckage. Inside: a letter from the
Admiral. "Every coin to the Fort. The Doctor says the Heart grows."
*You:* a ship battle against a tougher galleon. *New:* the Spanish galleon (more guns, more
crew, boards you if you get close).

**5. Wren's Vigil.** Wren feels three more captains stirring in their graves. Tonight, before
the Spanish get to them: three bounties on three islands, skulls back to the Order by dawn.
*You:* a three-island bounty against the clock (the night). *New:* night-only missions.

**6. The Sergeant's Camp** *(Act I finale)*. The letters lead to the big dig on Cutlass Cay's
hill: palisades, tents, a green bonfire raising a whole company of Spanish dead, and **Sargento
Calavera** in charge. Storm the camp. Douse the bonfire (three buckets) to stop more rising, then
take on the Sergeant: his tower shield stops anything from the front, so one of you keeps him
busy while another gets round him, or a pistol shot staggers him and drops the shield for a
moment. On his body: "All gold to Fuerte del Cuervo."
*You:* the first big fight, waves of skeletons, a boss. *New:* camps that keep raising dead
until their fire is out; the shield boss.
*Reward:* title **Gravewarden**; the Sergeant's halberd as a trophy for your ship's wall.

### Act II: The Raven's Due

*Pirate level 5. Who is doing this, and why. A deserter, a fort, a vault of gold.*

**7. A Deserter at Dawn.** A Spanish rowing boat drifts toward Port Merrow at sunrise, chased by
skeleton boats. In it: **Isabel Montero**, alive, bleeding, furious. Fight off the boats, get her
aboard your ship, and bring her to Martha at the Drowned Rat. If your ship sinks with her aboard,
the dead take her and you try again.
*You:* a rescue at sea. *New:* someone standing on your deck you must keep alive; skeleton
rowing boats.

**8. The Admiral's Story.** At the tavern Isabel tells it all: the treasure fleet, the hurricane
on the Black Reef, the boy who lived, the Doctor, the Heart. Her own captain refused to dig up
graves; Cuervo shot him and raised him. She needs his logbook from their sunk ship, the *San
Telmo*, under Gull Isle's arch. Dive down to the wreck (watch your breath), get the book.
*You:* a long talk, then a dive. *New:* diving with a breath bar, sunken wrecks.

**9. Starve the Fire.** Isabel knows the gold convoys: a treasure galleon and two sloops sail
from Cutlass Cay to the Fort. Silas is very keen. Sink the sloops, board the galleon, and carry
its gold chests across to your own ship before it goes down.
*You:* a convoy fight and a boarding raid. *New:* convoys, chests on an enemy deck.

**10. The Drowned Bells.** The dead are called by bells. Wren hears three of them: green bronze
bells the Spanish have hung on Parrot Rock's stacks, in Gull Isle's arch and up a palm on
Cutlass Cay. Every ring raises more dead. Shoot them down.
*You:* find and shoot three targets while each one summons more skeletons. *New:* things you
shoot down.

**11. Spies in Merrow.** Quill's ledger doesn't add up: salt is going missing from the pier.
Somebody in Merrow is selling to the Spanish. Ask about (Martha, the dockhand, Jonah), find a
raven coin hidden in a crate, and catch **Fat Gideon** at his cove on Gull Isle, handing salt to
the dead. He runs; they don't.
*You:* a short whodunit: talk, search, chase, fight. *New:* clue steps (talk to several people,
search crates).

**12. Fuerte del Cuervo** *(Act II finale)*. Isabel draws the fort: walls on a rock, cannons on
the towers, a gate, a vault. Sail in under the guns, blow the gate with a powder keg, and fight
through the courtyard against Spanish musketeers. On the walls waits **El Polvorín**, who
throws lit bombs (move, or kick them back at him if you are quick). Down in the cells: living
Spanish sailors who said no to the Admiral; let them out. In the vault: mountains of gold and the
truth. The Heart isn't here. It's on the Admiral's flagship, and the fleet rises "the night the
storm sits on the Black Reef".
*You:* the first fort battle: shore guns, a gate, ranged enemies, a boss, a vault full of chests.
*New:* the fort, shore cannons, musketeer skeletons, the bomb boss.
*Reward:* title **Fort-Breaker**; the fort becomes a world event (it re-garrisons now and then,
with a fresh vault).

### Act III: The Siege of Merrow

*Pirate level 10. The Spanish strike back at the port.*

**13. Ashes on the Wind.** Grey ash drifts over Merrow, and Moll's lighthouse burns green: the
Spanish have poisoned the lamp so their ships can find the harbour in the dark. Climb the
lighthouse, douse the green fire, carry lamp oil up from the tavern and light the true flame.
*You:* a climb, a douse, a carry. *New:* inside the lighthouse.

**14. Powder and Shot.** Merrow needs guns of its own. Quill has bought four old cannons from the
abandoned camp on Cutlass Cay (he got a very good price; nobody asked the Sergeant). Carry them and
their powder to Merrow's headland while skeleton sloops raid you on the way.
*You:* a heavy cargo run under attack. *New:* the shore battery at Merrow (players can fire it).

**15. Saltshot.** Wren and Isabel cook up a cannonball for the dead: packed with sea salt, it
bursts a skeleton ship's planks like dry bread. They need salt (Quill), the green bronze of the
drowned bells (Act II) and a barrel of brine from the edge of the Maw, where the sea is saltiest.
Sail to the Black Reef's edge, through its endless storm, and fill the barrel.
*You:* three gathers, one of them in the storm. *New:* **saltshot**: a cannonball that hurts the
dead more, and does nothing special to players (so it stays fair).

**16. The Pressed Dead.** Wren: "They've dug up Hollowbell." Captain Hollowbell was the best
pirate the Isles ever had, and now he leads Spanish dead. A big bounty: Hollowbell, three
Spanish officers, a camp. When his skull is in your arms, it talks: grateful, chatty, rude about
your sailing. Before Wren lays him to rest he tells you where the *Cuervo Negro* anchors at night
and that the Admiral means to burn Merrow.
*You:* a big bounty. *New:* talking skulls (they chat while you carry them).

**17. The Last Night of Peace.** Martha opens the tavern for everyone. Isabel raises a glass to
the crews of Merrow. Then the bell rings: raven sails on the horizon. Get the port ready:
barricades on the pier, powder to the battery, buckets by the tavern.
*You:* a story beat, then quick jobs that set up the battle. *New:* barricades you carry and place.

**18. The Siege of Port Merrow** *(Act III finale, call to arms)*. Night. Three galleons and the
*Cuervo Negro* come for the port. Every crew on the server is called.
1. **The bay:** hold the harbour. Ships and the shore battery sink the galleons.
2. **The beach:** the dead land in boats. Hold the pier and the tavern.
3. **La Viuda:** from the flagship's fighting top, the Widow picks off defenders one by one.
   Shoot her down, or climb aboard and get her.
4. **The Doctor:** Ocaso steps ashore in green fire, and the fallen get back up. He grabs
   Isabel, and the flagship pulls away.
Merrow stands, scorched. Isabel is gone.
*You:* the first multi-crew battle, in phases. *New:* call to arms, wave battles, defending a
place, the sniper boss.
*Reward:* title **Defender of Merrow**; a scorched Merrow flag for your ship.

### Act IV: The Drowned Fleet

*Pirate level 14. Taking the fight to them.*

**19. The Prison Hulk.** Hollowbell said it: the Admiral keeps prisoners in an old hulk in the fog
off Isla Ceniza. Slip in quietly. Lantern-carrying guards raise the alarm if they see you, and an
alarm brings skeleton ships. Find the jailer, take his keys, free Isabel, get out.
*You:* sneak (or don't), rescue, escape. *New:* guards who raise an alarm, fog.

**20. The Pilot's Chart.** Only one channel runs safely into the Black Reef, and only the drowned
fleet's pilot knew it. He's buried on **Isla Calavera**, the skull-shaped rock, with his chart.
Isabel remembers the riddle sung about him; work through it, landmark by landmark.
*You:* a full riddle hunt across a new island. *New:* Isla Calavera, riddle maps.

**21. Ghost Lights.** The fleet is waking ship by ship. In storms, a drowned galleon rises with
water pouring off it and hunts whoever is near. Find the storm, find the ghost, sink it again
(saltshot helps), while the lightning does its best to sink you.
*You:* a ship battle inside the storm. *New:* ghost galleons (rise from below, sink back).

**22. Lanterns of the Deep.** Ocaso has set three great green lanterns on sea stacks around the
Reef to light the drowned fleet's way up. Each sits on a rock with a garrison. Sail between them
under fire, land, smash each lantern.
*You:* three small landings, against the clock as the storm closes. *New:* lantern targets.

**23. The Merchant's Gambit.** Quill's finest hour. He fills crates with lead painted gold and
lets slip that a treasure ship is leaving Merrow. You are the treasure ship. The *Cuervo Negro*
takes the bait; lead it out of the Reef's channel and into Isabel's ambush, without getting sunk
or caught on the way.
*You:* a chase, being the bait. *New:* the enemy flagship chasing you; Isabel's ship fights
beside you for the first time.

**24. The Battle of the Black Reef** *(Act IV finale, call to arms)*. The storm sits on the
Reef. The drowned fleet rises, galleon after galleon breaking the surface. Every crew is called.
Sink the risen ships and break the *Cuervo Negro* until she's crippled and dead in the water.
Board her. On the quarterdeck, the **Raven Admiral** fights you himself: a duellist who parries
cuts from the front and lunges, with his marines around him. He falls overboard as his flagship
sinks. In the smoke, a rowing boat slips away: Ocaso, with the Heart blazing in his arms.
*You:* a fleet battle, boarding the flagship, a duel. *New:* the duellist boss, risen ships.
*Reward:* title **Raven-Breaker**; the Admiral's raven figurehead.

### Act V: The Heart of Ash

*Pirate level 18. The end.*

**25. What the Admiral Knew.** Dive to the wreck of the *Cuervo Negro* and find the Admiral's
cabin. His journal tells the last secret: Ocaso never served Spain. He hears the Heart speak,
and it wants every soul the sea ever drowned to walk. "Then it goes back," says Wren. "Back to
the Maw."
*You:* a deep dive into a big wreck. *New:* nothing; the story turns.

**26. Isla Ceniza.** The ash island: grey trees, a smoking hill, ash falling like snow. Land with
lanterns lit, fight through the ashen dead (they burst into green flame when they fall), and find
Ocaso's workshop in the hill. **Doctor Ocaso** jumps between three braziers in bursts of green
fire, raising the dead wherever he lands. Douse his braziers (bucket) and he can be hurt. He dies
laughing: the Heart isn't here. It's gone home to the Maw, and the dead are answering.
*You:* a new island, a magic boss. *New:* Isla Ceniza, ashen skeletons, the teleporting boss.

**27. Every Hand.** One last push needs everyone. Three jobs, one per company: carry saltshot from
Quill's warehouse to every ship in the port; light the beacon on each island to call every crew
in the Isles; and bring Wren's lantern to the edge of the Black Reef, where it will show where the
Heart lies. Isabel and her freed Spanish sailors (from the fort cells) ready their ship.
*You:* three short jobs across the map. *New:* beacons.

**28. The Ash Admiral.** At the Maw the Heart has raised the Admiral again, burned black and
crowned with green fire: the **Ash Admiral**, master of the whole drowned fleet at last. Break
the fleet before it can sail on Merrow.
*You:* the biggest sea battle in the game. *New:* everything the fleet has, at once.

**29. The Bone Colossus** *(the finale, call to arms)*. The Heart pulls every bone of the drowned
fleet together into a giant that rises out of the Maw: **the Bone Colossus**.
1. **At sea:** it wades the reef, smashing masts with its fists and throwing wreckage. Its four
   glowing joints (shoulders and knees) take saltshot. Break all four.
2. **On the reef:** it falls to its knees on the rocks. Land. The Ash Admiral stands on its
   ribs, guarding the Heart. Beat him for good.
3. **The Heart:** someone has to pick it up. It is heavy and it burns (you lose health while you
   carry it, and can't fight). The crews guard the carrier to the edge of the Maw while every dead
   thing in the sea comes for them. Throw it in.
The whirlpool closes. The green light goes out. Every skeleton on the server crumbles at once.
*You:* the final boss and the final carry. *New:* the Colossus.
*Reward:* title **Heartbreaker**; the Admiral's raven cloak; a green-fire lantern for your ship.

### Epilogue: Quiet Waters

**30. Quiet Waters.** Back at the Drowned Rat, the whole port celebrates. Isabel takes command of
Fuerte del Cuervo, which becomes a second friendly port (with its own buyers). Silas counts his
gold, Quill his crates, and Wren looks at the sea a long time. "The Heart wasn't the only thing
it keeps," she says. Skeletons still turn up here and there; the sea remembers.
*You:* a party, a last talk with everyone. *Unlocks:* the post-story world (below).

## Tales: side stories from Port Merrow

Shorter, lighter stories. Each opens once the chapter in brackets is done.

### Captain Coco (Jonah Salt) [after P1]

- **T1. Coconut Thieves.** The crabs of Gull Isle have stolen Captain Coco. Chase them across the
  beach; the one with the coconut is very fast and very smug.
- **T2. A Raft for Jonah.** Jonah wants to go home, wherever that is. Carry planks from three
  wrecks so he can build a raft. He builds it, sits on it, and decides he likes the island.
  *Reward:* Captain Coco's cousin, a coconut for your ship's wheel.

### Quill's Ledger (Abel Quill) [after chapter 2]

- **T3. The Missing Tea.** Twenty crates of Merrow's finest tea never arrived. Follow a trail of
  floating crates to a wreck, and collect what's left.
- **T4. Señor Hueso.** One crate has a skeleton in it: a raised Spanish cook who never got his
  orders and would very much like to keep cooking. He's polite. Deliver him (and the tea) to
  Jonah's island, where he opens a beach kitchen. A skeleton who doesn't fight back.
- **T5. The Governor's Ball.** Quill promised the tea to a ball by tonight. A timed cargo run with
  a fragile load: crates break if you drop them.
  *Reward:* title **Purveyor of Fine Goods**; a tea chest for your hold.

### Penny's Hoard (Silas Penny) [after chapter 16]

- **T6. My Boy Silas.** Hollowbell's talking skull mentions a hoard he left "for my cabin boy,
  little Silas Penny". Silas goes very quiet. Hollowbell's riddle starts on Parrot Rock.
- **T7. The Old Crew.** The riddle ends at a cave where Hollowbell's old crew have been raised to
  guard the hoard. Fight them; one of them remembers Silas, and stops fighting.
- **T8. Silas Cries.** Bring the hoard to Silas. He cries. It isn't about Hollowbell; it's about
  how lovely the gold is. (It's a bit about Hollowbell.)
  *Reward:* Silas's old cabin-boy cap.

### The Other Lantern (Mother Wren) [after chapter 18]

- **T9. Two Lights.** In the siege, a second green lantern moved among the dead. Wren knows it:
  her twin sister Linnet, drowned ten years ago, raised by Ocaso as his lantern-bearer.
- **T10. Linnet.** Follow Linnet's light across three islands at night. She blinds anyone who
  looks straight at her lantern; shoot the lantern out of her hand.
- **T11. Rest.** Bring Linnet's skull home. Wren lays her to rest at the edge of the sea.
  *Reward:* title **Lantern-Bearer**; a twin of Wren's lantern for your belt.

## The board missions as the story moves on

The three companies' repeatable missions never stop, and they change with your act so the war
is felt between chapters:

| Act | The Gilded Hand (treasure) | The Lantern Order (skulls) | The Merrow Traders (cargo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prologue, I | Hoards (as today), now and then with Spanish diggers | Bounties (as today) | Cargo (as today), salt and oil among the goods |

| III | Recover gold from the fort's vault | Lay the pressed dead to rest | Supply runs to Merrow's battery, raided on the way |
| IV, V | Wreck gold from the Reef's edge | Ghost-ship bounties: sink it, fish the captain's skull from the sea | Saltshot runs to ships at sea |

## Levelling up, gold and what you can buy

**Pirate level** (1 to 30) comes from XP. It shows on your name and earns titles and looks.
It never adds health, damage or speed. Rough numbers, to be tuned in play:

| Gets you XP | XP |
|---|---|
| A skeleton felled | 10 |
| A Spanish soldier or musketeer | 15 |
| A captain or officer | 50 |
| A boss | 250 |
| Another player knocked down | 25 (once per player per 5 minutes, so nobody farms friends) |
| A skeleton ship sunk | 150 |
| A Spanish galleon or ghost galleon sunk | 250 |
| A player ship sunk | 200 |
| A board mission finished | 150, plus 50 per chest, skull or crate |
| A story chapter | 300 to 600; act finales 1,000 |
| A world event | 300 to 800 |

Level n to n+1 needs 300 + 100 x n XP: level 5 at 2,200 XP, level 10 at 7,200, level 18 at
about 20,000, level 30 at about 52,000.

**Company standing** (the reputation we have today) stays per company: higher standing, bigger
and better-paid offers. **Pirate level** is the player's own and goes with them to any crew.

**Gold** (per player, decision 2) buys, at Port Merrow:

| Shop | Sells | Fair because |
|---|---|---|
| Tailor (Martha's back room) | Hats, coats, shirts, colours, eyepatches, peg legs | Looks only |
| Shipwright (on the pier) | Sail colours, flags, hull paint, figureheads, lanterns | Looks only |
| Armourer (Isabel, after Act II) | **Weapons as choices**: cutlass (all-round), rapier (fast, light, long reach), boarding axe (slow, heavy, short), pistol (all-round), blunderbuss (deadly close, useless far), musket (long range, slow to reload). **Armour as trade-offs**: padded coat (less sword damage, slower), light boots (faster, more damage taken), and so on. | Each has a weakness; nothing is simply better |
| Chandler (Quill) | Supplies: planks, cannonballs, saltshot (after Act III), food | Everyone can buy the same |

Story rewards (titles, the halberd, the raven cloak) can't be bought: you were there.

**Deeds**: long goals with a title or a look at the end, e.g. *douse 50 green fires*,
*sink 25 Spanish galleons*, *lay 100 skulls to rest*, *deliver 200 crates*, *carry the Heart*.

Technical notes:

- **Saving.** The server writes one JSON file per pirate name (level, XP, gold, titles, deeds,
  chapters done, owned looks) and company standing per crew name. No accounts: among friends a
  name is enough. A secret token per name stops someone else using it by accident.
- **Chapters are data,** not code: a list of steps, each one of a small set of kinds (talk, go
  to, douse, dig, defeat, carry to, deliver, pour, shoot, survive waves, dive, find clue). New
  chapters mostly need new data and, now and then, one new step kind.
- **Limits for big battles:** at most about 30 skeletons and 6 enemy ships alive at once on the
  server; waves wait for room. Bosses are skeletons with extra rules, like today's captain.
- **One mission at a time** per crew, story or board. The call to arms is the exception: any crew
  can join a battle without dropping its mission.
