# Milestone 6: missions

Status: written 07-10-2026 after Mark said "then on to the missions!". Every choice below is
Claude's default; change anything and the code follows.

## In one paragraph

Three trading companies give the crew missions. At the map table you choose one of three
offers, one from each company: a **treasure voyage** for the Gold Hoarders (dig up chests on
one to three islands), a **bounty** for the Order of Souls (hunt a named skeleton captain and
bring back his glowing skull) or a **cargo run** for the Merchant Alliance (carry crates from
Port Merrow's pier to a trader on another island before time runs out). Finishing a mission
pays gold and earns that company's reputation; the higher your standing, the bigger and better
paid its offers. Each company has someone at Port Merrow who buys what it wants.

## Glossary

| Word | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Company | Gold Hoarders, Order of Souls or Merchant Alliance. |
| Offer | A mission a company proposes at the map table: its kind, where, how big, roughly what it pays. |
| Mission | The offer the crew took. One at a time per crew (as voyages were). |
| Reputation | Points a crew earns with each company; every 300 is a level, up to level 5. |
| Bounty | A named skeleton captain on an island, with his crew. He drops his skull when he falls. |
| Skull | A glowing captain's skull you carry like a chest and sell to the Order of Souls. |
| Cargo | Crates (rum, silk, spice) you carry from Port Merrow's pier to the trader on another island. |
| Trader | A castaway trader who appears on the cargo run's island and takes delivery. |

## What players do

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| See the offers | E at the map table: a board with three offers. 1, 2 or 3 takes one; E or Esc closes it. |
| Read the mission | Hold M: the map shows every island still to visit, with an X, a skull or a crate on it. |
| Treasure | As before, an X per island. Chests sell to the Gold Hoarder (the merchant at his stall). |
| Bounty | Sail to the island, beat the captain (he is tougher than his crew), carry his skull to the Order's mystic at Port Merrow. |
| Cargo | Pick up each crate at Port Merrow's pier, sail them to the trader, E to hand one over. |
| Give up | Hold E at the map table for 2 s: the mission is dropped (no reputation lost). |

## Rules

- **Offers** are made fresh each time the board opens with no mission running. Each company's
  offer is sized by the crew's level with it:

  | Level | Treasure islands | Bounty crew | Cargo crates | Pay |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 | x1.0 |
  | 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 | x1.2 |
  | 3 | 2 | 3 | 2 | x1.4 |
  | 4 | 2 | 4 | 3 | x1.6 |
  | 5 | 3 | 4 | 3 | x2.0 |

- **Treasure**: one chest per island, each worth 80-320 gold times the pay factor. Selling every
  chest of the voyage finishes it.
- **Bounty**: the captain has 200 health, swings harder (30) and slower; his crew are ordinary
  skeletons. His skull is worth 250-450 gold times the pay factor.
- **Cargo**: each crate pays 120-200 gold times the pay factor on delivery. The run has a time
  limit (6 minutes plus 2 per crate); late crates pay half. A crate that falls in the sea is
  soaked and pays half. Delivering every crate finishes the run.
- **Reputation**: finishing a mission earns 150 points with its company plus 50 per item; any
  chest sold also earns 30 with the Gold Hoarders (also from skeleton ships). Level =
  1 + points / 300, at most 5.
- Other crews can steal chests, skulls and crates, and sell them for their own reputation; the
  mission ends for the crew that took it once all its items are gone.
- The mission card (top left) shows the company, the steps left and, for cargo, the time.

## New things to see

Order of Souls mystic (hooded, with a lantern) and Merchant Alliance agent at Port Merrow; the
castaway trader on the cargo island; the skeleton captain (bigger, in a coat and a feathered
hat, with a green lantern); the skull; cargo crates in three kinds; a mission board at the map
table. All made in Blender like the rest.

## Testing aids

`--mission KIND` (server) makes the first offer of that kind and the board pick it, for bots
and screenshots: `treasure`, `bounty` or `cargo`. `--rep N` starts every crew at level N.

## Done when

2. Smoke tests: a bot takes a bounty, the captain falls, the skull is sold; a bot takes a cargo
   run and delivers a crate; the treasure smoke test still passes through the new board.
3. All earlier smoke tests pass.
