## In one paragraph

Act III, from pirate level 10: the Spanish strike back at Port Merrow. The crews relight Moll's
poisoned lighthouse, haul cannons from the Sergeant's old camp to a battery on Merrow's beach,
brew saltshot (cannonballs that tear the dead's ships apart), lay the great pirate Captain
Hollowbell to rest (his skull talks all the way home), barricade the pier on the last quiet
night, and then hold the port against the Admiral's fleet: three galleons in the bay, the dead
landing on the beach, La Viuda's musket from the flagship, and Doctor Ocaso himself, who takes
Isabel. Every crew on the server is called to the fight.

## Rules

- **Lighthouse**: E at the door puts you on the gallery, E on the gallery brings you down; a
  bucket of water or the jar of oil you carry goes with you. The lamp's green fire is a story fire
  (one bucket); while it burns the lighthouse glows and sweeps green, by day as well as night.
- **Hauling cannons**: each cannon is carried like a chest (both arms), and can be stowed on deck.
  A skeleton ship is sent at the crew every 2 minutes during the chapter, one at a time.
- **Battery**: once chapter 14 is done, four guns stand on the beach for that crew's siege.
  Each fires every 6 s at the nearest enemy ship within 220 m while any pirate is within 12 m.
- **Saltshot**: a cannonball from a crew with saltshot that holes a skeleton or Spanish ship holes
  it twice.
- **Talking skull**: a line every 14 s to whoever carries it (as a message, so it never gets in
  the way).
- **The siege**: three galleons appear 300 m off Merrow; when they are sunk, the dead land in
  three waves of six on Merrow's beach (the next wave when at most one is left); then La Viuda
  (150 health) fires every 4 s at the nearest pirate within 70 m, 40 damage, after a 1 s glint;
  then Ocaso raises four dead on the pier and leaves with Isabel when they fall. Everyone within
  200 m of Merrow at the end gets 300 XP and 150 gold, whichever crew they sail with.
- **Isabel** is gone from the tavern after the siege (until Act IV).
- **Act gate**: Act III needs pirate level 10 (the best in the crew).
