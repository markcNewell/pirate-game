# Art pass 1

Status: done 07-10-2026 while Mark was away, after milestone 5. Every choice below is Claude's
default. Everything is still built in code (no Blender models yet), in the toon style with ink
outlines chosen at the start.

## What changed, by where you see it

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Title screen | A live scene behind the menu: the galleon sailing at golden hour. |
| Sky and light | A 24-minute day on the shared server clock: sunrise, golden hour, sunset glow, a moon, twinkling stars, blue nights. Lanterns, pier lamps, camp fires and the lighthouse come on at dusk. |
| Weather | One storm roams the map: a dark cloud bank over a curtain of rain from afar; grey light, closing fog, rain and lightning inside; on a bright day a rainbow stands in front of it. Scenery only for now (no effect on waves or ships). |
| Sea | Bright shallows with caustic ripples, foam rings on beaches, wakes, a bow wave, sun glints, ripple lines close by, cloud shadows drifting across, a glitter path toward a low sun or the moon. The sea now cuts out the hulls of the nearest 8 ships (was 4). |
| Ships | Planked hulls, sail panels, crew sail colours and emblem, gold name lettered on the stern, fluttering flag, sails that breathe in gusts, bow spray at speed, a hold with frames and hammocks, stern windows lit at night, cannonball piles, gulls round the mainmast. Skeleton ships: torn sails and a green mist. |
| Sinking | Bubbles over a sinking ship, then planks and barrels floating where she went down. |
| Combat | Smoke trails behind cannonballs, an orange flash of light per shot. |
| People | Pirates and skeletons walk with swinging legs and arms; other players hold their cutlass or pistol, swing it, and nod their heads with where they look. Name tags keep one size on screen. Four skin tones. Skeletons' eyes glow green. Your own fist and sleeve hold your weapon, and a cutlass swing leaves a white swoosh. |
| Islands | Bushes, flowers, grass patches, skeleton camps, gulls, beach wrecks on the bigger islands, a rock arch on Gull Isle, sea stacks off Parrot Rock. |
| Port Merrow | A striped lighthouse with sweeping beams at night, a tavern with chimney smoke and windows lit from dusk, a merchant who watches you. |
| Ambience | Dolphins leaping alongside a ship under way, a whale spouting far off, wind lines drifting downwind, crabs scuttling on the beaches, fireflies over the islands at night. |
| HUD | Dark-wood panels with gold edges, a pill compass with blackletter letters, rounded bars, and a gold ring round the crosshair that fills while you hold E to patch or dig. |
| Treasure map | Torn, scorched parchment with sea marks, palms, a dotted trail to the X and a compass rose. |
| Falling | The world seen drained and cold through a ghostly veil until you are back aboard. |

## Also fixed along the way

- Gunner bots could pace back and forth beside a cannon forever; a bot now stays put once it
  reaches a station. The crew-vs-crew broadside smoke test had been failing on this branch.
- `docs/` is no longer imported by Godot, so README screenshots stay out of exports.
- Swimming: the camera sits half a metre higher (the body and reach checks do not move), so
  passing crests no longer fill the screen.

## Tools for looking at it

`tools/screenshot.sh OUT VIEW [args]` renders one frame without a monitor. New views: `stern`,
`flag`, `swim`, `tavern`, `lighthouse`, `ghost`, `swing`, `patch`, `island1`..`island3` (with `--shot-from X,Y,Z`). New
options: `--time T` (0 midnight, 0.5 noon, 0.79 sunset), `--storm X,Z`, `--shot-yaw Y`, `--dolphins`,
`--whale`.
