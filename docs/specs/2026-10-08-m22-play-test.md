# Milestone 22: Play-test from the title screen

Status: built 08-10-2026. Thirty-two story chapters, eleven tales and seven world events, and
nobody has played them with a keyboard and mouse yet. This lets Mark (or a friend) jump straight
to any of them from the title screen instead of playing hours to get there. Every choice below is
Claude's default; change anything and the code follows.

## In one paragraph

Under the sound sliders on the title screen, a "Play-test" switch opens three options: start at
any chapter (the prologue to Quiet Waters, and every tale), start with some gold, and start with a
world event under way. Host & play then starts a game set up that way: the chapter is under way
for crew 1, the ship is by the island it sends you to, your pirate has done every chapter before
it and is the level its act asks for. A play-test game never saves your pirate, so your real
progress is untouched.

## Rules

- **What "before it" means**: for a story chapter, every story chapter before it; for a tale, the
  chapter it follows and everything before that (no other tales).
- **Level**: the level the chapter's act asks for (1 for the prologue and Act I, then 5, 10, 14,
  18), so the next chapters are offered as they would be.
- **Where the ship starts**: off the island the chapter sends you to; at the Black Reef's edge for
  the chapters out there; at the usual spawn when the chapter starts at Port Merrow.
- **Crew**: the chapter is crew 1's, so you host into crew 1 (friends who join pick "Any crew").
- **Saving**: never. Close the game and your pirate is as it was.
- **Gold**: 0 to 20,000, in steps of 500. **World event**: none, or any of the seven but the
  ghost galleon (that one comes when you sail deep into the storm).
- The same options as command-line flags for tests: `--chapter ID --jump --done IDS --xp N
  --gold N --event NAME --no-save`.

## Done when

1. Unit tests: what each chapter needs done; the options (never saved, the right level); jumping
   into a chapter starts it with everything before it done and the ship by its island; every
   chapter can be jumped to; every event can be started.
2. A screenshot of the title screen with the options open, fitting a 720-line screen.
3. All earlier tests and smoke tests pass.
