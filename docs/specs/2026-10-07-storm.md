# The storm

Status: written 07-10-2026 after Mark asked for "art and sound first along with the storm".
Every choice below is Claude's default.

## In one paragraph

The roaming storm stops being scenery. Inside it the sea heaves with much bigger waves, the
wind swirls round its heart and gusts, and lightning now and then strikes a ship's mainmast,
holing the hull and hurting anyone standing by the mast. Sail round it, or ride it out with a
crew on the pumps.

## Glossary

| Word | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Storm | The one storm that loops round the map on the shared clock (`Storm.centre`). |
| Heart | Within 0.7 of the storm's radius of its centre, where it is at full strength. |
| Strength | 0 outside the storm, rising to 1 at the heart (`Storm.amount`). |
| Gust | The wind's strength inside the storm, wandering between half and one and a half. |

## Rules

- **Waves**: heights grow with strength, up to 2.6 times as high at the heart. The server
  floats ships on the same formula the sea shader draws, so what you see is what you sail.
- **Wind**: inside the storm the wind turns to swirl anticlockwise round the centre, up to 70%
  of the way at the heart, and its push on the sails gusts between 0.5 and 1.5 times.
- **Lightning**: every flash has a one-in-three chance to strike the ship nearest the bolt
  among those deep in the storm (strength over a half). A strike holes the hull below the
  mainmast and does 25 damage to anyone within 4 m of the mast foot. Everyone sees the bolt
  hit the mast; the crew are told.
- Whether a flash strikes, and where every other bolt falls, follows from the clock alone, so
  all players see the same storm.

## Testing aids

- `--calm` (server): no storm effects. The existing smoke tests use it so the storm's path
  over Parrot Rock in the first minutes does not change what they check.
- `--storm X,Z` pins the storm (now on the server too); the server tells joining clients
  whether the storm is on and where it is pinned, so their waves match.
