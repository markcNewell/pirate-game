# Milestone 15: Deeds

Status: built 09-10-2026. The story doc's "Deeds" (docs/specs/2026-10-08-story.md): long goals
with a title at the end. Every number below is Claude's default; change anything and the code
follows.

## In one paragraph

Every pirate keeps a tally of what they have done on the sea, saved with their profile and
counted whatever crew they sail with. Reach a deed's goal and you earn its title (which you wear
at once, and can be seen on your name). Press J to read your deeds: how far along each one is.

## The deeds

| Deed | Goal | Title |
|---|---|---|
| Green fires doused (story or world event) | 50 | Firewatch |
| The dead felled (any skeleton, by your hand) | 500 | Bonebreaker |
| Spanish galleons sunk (by your crew) | 25 | Scourge of the Raven |
| Ghost galleons sunk (by your crew) | 10 | Ghost-Sinker |
| Skulls laid to rest with Wren (sold) | 100 | Friend of the Lantern |
| Chests sold to Silas | 100 | Silas's Favourite |
| Crates delivered | 200 | Merrow's Own Merchant |
| The Heart carried into the Maw | 1 | Heart-Carrier |

Ships sunk count for everyone in the crew that sank them; the rest count for the pirate who did it.
Titles from deeds, like story titles, can't be bought. Looks as deed rewards are left for later.
