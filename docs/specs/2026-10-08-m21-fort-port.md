# Milestone 21: The fort as a port

Status: built 08-10-2026, with a scenario in `tests/smoke/shipwright_test.sh` (bot `fortarms`,
test hook `--done CHAPTERS`). The story doc's world after the story: Fuerte del Cuervo becomes a
second friendly port. Every choice below is Claude's default; change anything and the code
follows.

## In one paragraph

Once someone in a crew has finished the story, Isabel Montero holds Fuerte del Cuervo for that
crew. She stands in the courtyard inside the gate with her rack of arms (the same blades, guns
and armour as Martha's, at the same prices), and three supply barrels by the gate load the
crew's ship like Port Merrow's. While the dead hold the fort (the M18 world event) the port is
shut: retake it first.

## Rules

- **Whose port**: a crew's, while anyone aboard has finished the story (chapter e6). Each pirate
  is told when that changes, and sees Isabel and the barrels only then.
- **Isabel's arms**: E by her opens the arms column of Martha's counter. Bought once, yours for
  good, wherever you bought it. Coats, hats and dyes are still Martha's alone.
- **The barrels**: E by one loads cannonballs, planks and food onto the crew's ship, if she is
  close enough (as at Port Merrow).
- **The dead come back**: during the Fuerte del Cuervo world event Isabel and the barrels are
  closed to everyone.
- Isabel is no longer at the Drowned Rat's door for that crew: she has a fort to run.

## Done when

1. Unit tests: open only after the story and for the whole crew, not another crew; shut while the
   dead hold it; Isabel sells arms but not coats; the barrels restock; each pirate is told; the
   port's places are inside the walls where you can stand.
2. A smoke test: a bot whose pirate has finished the story goes in through the gate and buys a
   rapier from Isabel.
3. All earlier tests and smoke tests pass; a screenshot of the courtyard.
