# Milestone 23: Music

Status: built 08-10-2026, at Mark's request ("free licensed tracks please"). Every track is
Kevin MacLeod's (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0; the
credits are on the title screen and in CREDITS.md. The choice of tracks, and when each plays, is
Claude's default: swap any track by replacing its file in `art/music` and its title in `Music`.

## In one paragraph

The score follows what is around you. A jig while you sail by day, a quiet Celtic tune at night,
something eerie in the storm and on the Black Reef, a skeletons' dance when the dead come close,
an orchestral battle when a skeleton ship is near, and the Danse Macabre for the story's bosses.
At Port Merrow the score stops and the Drowned Rat's band plays from the tavern. A level gained
gets a short fanfare over whatever is playing.

## When each plays

| Mood | When | Track |
|---|---|---|
| Title | The title screen | Celtic Impulse |
| Day | Sailing by day, nothing about | Fiddles McGinty, then Achaidh Cheide (a rest of about 50 s between) |
| Night | Sailing at night | Errigal (a rest of about 60 s between) |
| Storm | In the roaming storm or the Black Reef's (Storm.amount 0.45 or more) | The Pyre |
| The dead | Living skeletons within 30 m of you | One-eyed Maestro |
| Battle | A skeleton ship within 230 m of your ship (not La Golondrina, not the prison hulk) | Five Armies |
| Boss | One of the story's bosses within 120 m of you, or the Bone Colossus within 450 m | Danse Macabre |
| Port | Within 70 m of Port Merrow's shore: the score stops | (the tavern plays Galway, heard near it) |
| Level gained | A short fanfare over the score, which ducks | Discovery Hit |

Danger comes first: boss, then battle, then the dead, then the storm, then port, then day or
night. Danger's music stays on 8-12 seconds after it has gone, so a lull doesn't flip the score.
A change fades the old track out over a few seconds as the new one fades in. The Music slider on
the title screen sets how loud all of it is.
