# Play-test checklist (about 30 minutes)

For the first play with a keyboard and mouse. Note anything that feels wrong, however small:
a line in a message or an issue is plenty. Numbers (XP, gold, damage, how many dead) are all
first guesses, so "too slow", "too easy" or "too much" is exactly what's wanted.

## 1. Alone, from the start (10 min)

Host & play, Play-test off.

- [ ] Walking, looking, jumping on deck and ashore: does the mouse feel right? (Esc opens the
      menu with mouse sensitivity.) Does anything stutter?
- [ ] Raise the anchor, set the sails, steer to Gull Isle. Is sailing fun on your own?
- [ ] The prologue (Moll at the lighthouse): is it clear what to do and where to go?
- [ ] Fight a few skeletons with the cutlass and the pistol. Too hard or too easy?
- [ ] Music and sound: too loud, too quiet, anything grating? (The Music slider is on the
      title screen.)
- [ ] Frame rate: smooth everywhere? Worst at sea, in the storm, or at Port Merrow?

## 2. Jumping around with Play-test (15 min)

Title screen: Play-test on, pick a chapter, Host & play. Try these three:

- [ ] **C6 The Siege of Port Merrow**: the busiest scene. Is it smooth, and is the battery
      fun?
- [ ] **D6 The Battle of the Black Reef**: a boss duel on the Cuervo Negro. Fair?
- [ ] **E5 The Bone Colossus**: the finale. Is breaking its joints with cannon satisfying?

Then: Play-test with 5,000 gold, buy something from Big Martha (tavern) and from Old Hobb (pier),
and check how it looks.

## 3. With a friend over the internet (if you can, 10 min)

- [ ] Host & play, and have them Join your address (forward port 24565 on your router), or run
      the Linux server download on a machine with a public address.
- [ ] Sail together: does their ship and their character move smoothly, or jerkily?
- [ ] Fight something together. Do hits land when they look like they should?
- [ ] **Voice**: Esc, check your mic moves the level bar (pick the right one under Microphone
      if not). Hold V and talk: you see the mic mark bottom left; they see a
      speaker over your head, and hear you from your pirate, fading as you walk apart and gone
      by about 40 m. Is it clear enough? Any echo (headphones help), crackle or lag?
- [ ] Try open mic (Esc, Voice). Does the gold level cut out the background but not your words?
- [ ] On a Mac: did it ask for the microphone the first time? (If you said no: System Settings >
      Privacy & Security > Microphone.)

## Worth noting

- Anything that made you stop and wonder what to do next.
- Anything that looked broken: floating, sinking into the ground, stuck skeletons.
- The thing you enjoyed most, and the most boring minute.
